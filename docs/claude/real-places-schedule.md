# Real places & schedule edits (spec 014)

Detail moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-11-02, the file passed Claude Code's size limit). Keep this file current when the feature changes.

Design: `specs/014-real-places/{spec,plan}.md` (built by three parallel builders: geo / sched / map).

- **Geo (places, routes, lookups, the venue rule)** — Projected places are authoritative: `places.js:worldWithPlaces` runs inside `reducer.worldOverlay` (every caller sees moved / event-created places and `world.routes`; the world itself by identity while nothing moved, so pre-014 fixtures are untouched; memo keys carry `placesKey` / `state.placesSig`, set only by a 014 fold). One geo client per process (`src/life/geo/client.js`, built in `src/index.js start()` → `startLife({geo})`; the facade default is `null` = `geo_off`; only the life facade and `src/index.js` import `src/life/geo/`): Nominatim search / Overpass "nearest <kind>" (QL from `geo/kinds.js` constants only) / OSRM routes; fixed `.env` hosts, `redirect:'error'`, ≤ 1 req/s per host (one serial queue), timeouts, JSON + byte caps, one `Retry-After` retry, an LRU, a daily cap; every failure a bare `GeoError(code)` (no URL, query, coordinate or email in errors or logs). Venue names are cleaned (`geo/clean.js`: format chars, NFKC, sanitizer, charset, no URL/@/JID/markers/real names, no phone-like run as the public scrubber sees it (any script's digits with spaces/brackets/dots/dashes) and no 7+ digits in all, `VENUE_BLOCKLIST`; > 5 words / 40 chars flagged for the owner's tick) and frozen at apply. Results enter the world only through the Director (`story/placesDirector.js`): `place.real` (a candidate → owner `place.created {venue}` with an opaque id `place:v<8 hex>`, or `place.updated {lat, lon, venue, prev}` of a generic place), `places.realify` (the "make places real" job's dry table), `routes.refresh`; preview resolves candidate / job-result ids ONCE into a frozen `entry.resolved` passed per call to the pure `plan()` and reused at apply (a bundle / retcon: every part's ids into the one map). Geo candidate ids are `cand:<uuid>` everywhere (the preview route's schedule shape check too). Never switched: his home, the family home, the office (career overlay too), cast homes, private kinds (`private_place`); a place he is at / travelling to-from is `in_use`. Owner-only, chat-forbidden `route.recorded {a<b, profile, km, minutes, aAt, bAt, pts ≤ 200 E5 ints}` (REWIND_KEEP; a newer record wins; used only while both endpoints sit within 50 m of `aAt`/`bAt`); `travel.planTrip` on a route = `roadKm / speed × 60 × peak + overhead`, cost `roadKm × perKm`, keys `roadKm/routed/routeKey/routeEventId` only when routed (copied into commute blocks and `travel.started`, R38); home-kind endpoints are sent at 3 dp and the stored geometry is straight within 400 m of them. `place.updated` / `place.created` are revertible (refused while in use / referenced). **No negative event at a named business** (§A7): `places.js` `NEGATIVE_TYPES` / `NEUTRAL_TYPES` (a guard covers `EVENT_TYPES`), `negativeAt` (from his position; a `decision.expired` or `outcome.happened` maps to its decision's place) / `negativeNow`, `placeLabel` / `safePlaceName` (a venue-like place — real venue or owner-renamed `ownerNamed` — near a negative event, or colliding with a real person's name, → its kind word), render-time scrubs of stored labels over `venueNamesEver()` (excluded events included; the facade's log reader, cached by lastEventId — its failure logs a warn), journal / narration charter line + `scrubNegativeVenue`, a per-turn mouth line only when a venue name is in the life tail, owner text `negative_at_venue`, weekly `new_place` refused on a venue name. Dashboard: Director PLACES panel (`components/placesPanel.js`). Tests: `life-tests/50–53`, `arcs-tests/50–53`. No migration (the log is the durable route cache; v14 if one is ever needed, never v13).
- **Schedule edits (sched)** — the owner changes his day from the Director (SCHEDULE panel, `components/schedule-panel.js`) or in words (TELL THE STORY): a day off / working from home / at the office (the whole day or from a time) plus ≤ 3 stretches (home, out at a place, gym, errand, work, sleep: sleep in / early night / nap), or remove / move one routine block, for today … +7 days. One action = one `schedule.override_set` (normalized by `planEdits.normalize`, folded into `s.scheduleOverrides[id]`, pruned 2 days after its date; ≤ 12 active, ≤ 4 a date, no overlap on a date). `src/life/planEdits.js` is pure: `fitAnchors` places an override into unix plan blocks (carve, leave in time, refill with free time where he is, trips repaired, meals moved, leisure in the way dropped with `override:<what>:<HHMM>` codes, an unfit override left out with `override:unfit:<id>`); `routine.dayType(…, {overrides})` returns `'off'` (planned like a weekend day with the weekend wake) / `'wfh'` / `'office'` for a whole-day background; `wakeTime` and `availability.routineAwake` honour a sleep-in; `planDay` fits the date's overrides and a next-day sleep-in ends tonight's sleep (R32). Today: `replanToday` splices into the plan in force (blocks before now, gear, workLate and everything outside the edit stay, R26); the pulse's `schedule` item (`PRIO 5.5`, `planEdits.scheduleDrift`) re-plans when the active overrides with a part still to come differ from `plan.overrides`, an inactive one's stretch is ahead (clear / revert / rewind → refilled from the routine), or a chat anchor's decision is gone; a committed non-story outing inside a new range expires `schedule` first (R21); undone re-plans stay undone (`schedule:<date>:<ids>` exclusion sig, R37). The story facade's `director.apply` settles the pulse right after a schedule apply (R36). Other planners treat override ranges as busy: `eveningProposal`, goals, the reconciler's fit (claims, invitations). A day off gets no deadline crunch and no interview / appraisal; a schedule cancellation costs no trust (`cast.cancelledSince` unchanged). Conflicts (`planEdits.conflictsOf`, half-open windows: a decision runs [eventAt, its `late` option's expiry = the outing's end) — never + a default 2 h when it has one; a range from the outing's end, or a `home` range over its trip back, is no conflict): a locked outing (`planEdits.lockedOuting` = `planWeight.isLockedOuting`: a committed go of weight promise or owner; 020: a promise he'll be late for also keeps by default, `heldPlan`) → keep by default; any other evening decision / invitation in a range → cancel (default) or keep; a fitted goal → reopen / keep; keep trims the range around the plan and splits it when the plan sits inside (pieces < 15 min dropped, ≤ `RANGES_MAX` longest kept; rows carry `at`, `locked`, `trimmed`); story-family decisions and commitments keep-only (`conflict_story` / `conflict_commitment` when inside), meetings dropped. `scheduleDrift`'s R21 expiry never touches a locked outing or a row kept at apply. Continuity (`ruleThreads`): every cancelled / reopened row → one HIGH `cancels_plan` "this cancels <label> at HH:MM" (own and chat-made separately; a chat plan is "a plan from a chat") carrying `keep: ['<type>:<id>']` (`cleanWarning` keeps it) → KEEP IT INSTEAD in the modal (`data-act="keep-instead"` → `keepInstead(action, keys)` re-previews), on the prompt draft card (`compactPreview` now shows continuity ≥ WARN, `ld-p-keep`) and in the SCHEDULE panel (`ld-s-keep`); a kept row whose range was trimmed → WARN `keeps_plan` (these two alone never auto-run the story read: `autoStoryRead`). Test: `arcs-tests/60-sched-locked-conflicts` (the live replay: outing 00:15–01:15 + home 01:15–02:30 kept). Refusals: `in_past`, `too_far`, `full`, `overlap` (names the other id), `during_travel`, `asleep_now`, `no_place`, `private_place`, `geo_off` (a `candidateId` without geo's `deps.venuePlace`), `sick`, `unfit`. The preview's projection is `tomorrow.simulate` with `descriptors` + `deadlineMs` (outside the fingerprint). Prompt (A6a): charter shapes for `schedule` / `schedule.clear`, the can't-do list says "changing his employer or job" (a day off / WFH is allowed), catalogue `schedule` (days 0–7: type, work, wake, sleep, overrides; trimmed first) and `overrides`; a range naming a real venue (`venueQuery`) makes a `needs_venue` draft (geo's post-step turns it into `needs_pick`); the planner's "would it fit?" trial folds a new venue's `place.created` / `route.recorded` first (else every new venue was `unfit`); a meal a range cuts is all or nothing (the longer piece outside the range stays the meal if it is at least the meal's minimum, else the whole meal moves); schedule drafts are never in APPLY ALL (`target_resolved`), `schedule.clear` is. Tests: `life-tests/55-sched-overrides`, `56-sched-replan`, `arcs-tests/55-sched-director`, `56-sched-prompt`.
- **Venue outings (prompt × geo)** — a scene outing may name a real business or landmark: the charter tells the model `"venue": {name, area}` (outing) / `"venueQuery"` (schedule range) and keeps `newPlace` / `new_place` for an invented generic place only. `schema.js sceneOf` takes exactly one of `placeId` / `newPlace` / `venue` (two → `bad_field`; a venue on a visit → `bad_field`); the query goes to the draft's `venues` slot (`{field:'place', query}`; a schedule's is `{range, query}`), never onto the action. `fillVenueDrafts` looks it up (→ `needs_pick` with candidates; a lookup failure or no geo stays `needs_venue`). The card's REAL PLACE FOR THE OUTING picks a candidate → `candidateId` on the scene (the route allows only `cand:<uuid>`, no venue data) → `resolveGeoAction` freezes it → `planOutingScene` reuses `venueRange` (geo's `venuePlace`: opaque `place:v<hex>`, an existing place with the same venue reused, `full`; region enforced by the search) with the venue's `place.created {venue}` / `route.recorded` caused by the change's `director.action`, then the outing at it — one apply, never in APPLY ALL (`target_resolved`); REVERT THIS CHANGE undoes both (a `route.recorded` naming a place the same change created rides along with it, `life.revertChangePreview` — so a `place.real` new venue is now revertible as a change too); without geo → `geo_off`; with no candidates the card offers his known places (`placeId`). **Safety net** (`looks_real_place`, warn): a `newPlace` / `new_place` whose name contains the name of a venue-like place he knows, any venue name ever recorded, or a known venue area (`place.venue.area`) — whole words, any case, ≥ 4 letters (`director.looksRealPlace`) → a planner continuity warning "this looks like a real place; use the place search"; the prompt's `flagLooksReal` adds the same text when the name is one the owner typed with a capital in the wish, or contains a venue query / candidate of that run (card warning, `reviewReason:'continuity'`). Prompt drops are logged at info as codes only (`drops:[{index, kind, reason}]` on `evt:'arcs.director.prompt'`; the route's `route_shape` drops in the `director.prompt` audit's `drops`); the Director page says each in plain words (`DROP_REASONS`). Test: `arcs-tests/58-venue-outing`.
- **Map (Leaflet on Life · Map and Life · Day, plan §B)** — Leaflet 1.9.4 is vendored byte-for-byte from the npm tarball (registry sha512 checked) as `src/dashboard/public/vendor/leaflet/1.9.4/{leaflet-src.esm.js,leaflet.css,LICENSE}` + `SHA256SUMS` (no marker images: circle markers only; the ESM's `.map` is not vendored); `test:dashboard` pins the hashes and checks they're served under auth. `components/leafmap.js` is the only module that loads it (dynamic `import()` + one `<link>` built with `el()`, nothing at import time, so pages still import in node) and the only Leaflet HTML sink: `textTooltip` binds a text node (`textContent`), the attribution is the server's constant (`routes/life.js` `OSM_ATTRIBUTION`, escaped, OSM linked to its copyright page) set once with `setPrefix(false)`; pages never call `bindPopup`/`bindTooltip`/`setContent`/`divIcon`/`addAttribution`/`tileLayer` (static check). Tiles: `LIFE_MAP_TILE_URL` (default OSM's tile server, so installs now load tiles; `''` = off), fetched by the viewer's browser with `referrerPolicy: 'origin'`; the map mounts once per visit and the 30 s poll only refills layers when places/trips change (`leafSig`), never remounts or cache-busts tiles. Fallback: tiles off or Leaflet failing to load → the page's inline SVG grid (`mapSvg`, which also draws `pts` and dashed planned trips). Data: `life.mapView({date})` (pure builder `src/life/mapView.js`; `mapData()` is unchanged for 013) → `GET /api/life/map[?date=YYYY-MM-DD]` (400 on a bad date) = `{running, date, from, to, places:[{id,name,kind,lat,lon,area,venue}], position, trips:[{from,to,mode,startAt,endAt,arrivedAt,eventId,planned?,pts?}], home:{placeId,lat,lon,radiusM:400}, tileUrl, attribution}`: date null = the last 48 h + the rest of today's plan's commute blocks (`planned:true`); a date = that local day (no position, planned trips only for today). Route geometry by data contract only (no geo import): `travel.started.payload.routeEventId` / a block's `routeEventId` → a not-undone `route.recorded` → `pts` (E5 ints, ≤ 200) / 1e5, reversed when the trip runs b → a; anything missing → a straight line, so a past trip keeps the geometry it was planned with (R38). `area` = `place.venue?.area ?? null`, `venue` = `!!place.venue`. Home is drawn as a 400 m area, not a pin. Tests: `life-tests/map-view.test.js` (builder, engine, route), the `map-*` block of `test:dashboard` (vendor, sinks, fallbacks with a fake Leaflet, guides).
