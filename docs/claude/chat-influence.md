What people say to him can touch his simulated day a little. Everything lives in `src/life/influence/`; **only the life facade (`src/life/index.js`) imports `src/life/influence/index.js`** (the projector imports the pure `influence/mood.js`; routes go through the facade; guard `00-guards`). Inert until the `chat-influence` skill is on and life runs: no model call, no event, no prompt change, the overlay closed — `01-inert` proves it byte for byte against golden fixtures recorded from the pre-012 base. Design: `specs/012-chat-influence/{spec,plan}.md`. P1: mood, sleep push, work counter; P2: invitations with maybe + follow-up; P3: advice → the owner's tray (all shipped, inert). Review fixes: plan.md's Review log.

- **Perception** — `processTurn` runs 009's matcher (`perceiveChat(…, {influence:false})`) before the gate; the gate's verdict hook in `applyLifeGate` (synchronous, before its first await; `'error'` on a failed gate) then calls the facade's `perceiveInfluence(msg, audience, ctx, verdict)`, which calls `influence.perceive` once `chatMayNudge` passed. Only now/sneak/defer may spend a Jev call — ignore/react/error count as `nothing:gate_<verdict>` (after the candidate filter, before the caps). `perceiveChat` without `influence:false` still perceives regardless (tests, `verdict: null`). Preconditions: a known canonical sender of a fresh group (or a DM sender in a fresh group), a text message ≥ 4 chars, not forwarded, not `no_learn`/disappearing, not while imitating, not a skill command, and a candidate — DM, @-mention, reply to him, name/alias, or a group he sent in < 10 min ago. Bursts: the newest candidate line. Caps (fail closed): per sender/hour, per chat/hour, per day = max(local attempts, ledger rows + in-flight) — a ledger read failure counts as reached. ONE typed Jev call (`life.chat.perceive`, fenced excerpt ≤ 600 + ≤ 3 labelled earlier lines, never stored): kind/target/affect/intensity/advice, each p ≥ 0.6 except a mood moment (kind `affect` and its affect: ≥ 0.5, owner 2026-10-31; intensity keeps 0.6, else 'low') and the target: P(him) + P(group_with_him) ≥ 0.6 (the effective target is the larger; `someone_else` ≥ 0.6 → nothing; `perceive.targetOf`). The result is HELD until `processTurn` reports the gate verdict (`life.gateOutcome`): now/sneak → applied; defer → a `held` row (codes only, keyed by the perceived line) applied by `phone.drainChat`'s `beforeDrainTurn` — every held row of that chat, since a debounced burst queues its last line while perception may have kept an earlier one (dated at read time) — or a later live read of that chat; anything else → dropped. Applying re-reads the message (prompt-visible, same sha256) in one synchronous section with the appends.
- **Mood** — an inert `chat.perceived` pointer (actor chat, the chat's origin, the sender as subject in a group) + an engine `mood.chat` caused by it (bounded delta × intensity × `moodScale` × same-sender damping 1/½/¼ within an hour). - **Chat load** (2026-10-31, area `mood`) — `afterTick`, live only, every ≥ 30 min: `messageStore.chatLoadBetween` counts his sent messages and the prompt-visible incoming ones in the chats he was talking in (a DM's forms once; ≤ 2 h after downtime; the cursor `chatLoad:cursor` in `chat_influence_state` seeds at the first tick, so history is never counted) → one counts-only, self-origin engine `chat.load {sent, received, chats, windowMin}` when he sent anything. `vitals.applyEvent`: social battery − min(8, 0.5 × sent + 0.1 × received) (R3's "each reply −0.5"), arousal up (≤ 0.1), valence + (battery ≥ 40) / − (battery < 25) ≤ 0.06. - **What an invitation's extraction can fail on (plan 020 H4)** — `resolvePlan` first tidies the clerk's answer (`story/inviteDrift.js`, this path only: the claim path and `validateExtraction` are untouched). What's clearly stated is normalised: `8pm`, `20:00:00`, `12am`, `this_evening`, `saturday`, a date of `tomorrow`. A bad optional field (minutes, place, summary, kind) becomes nothing; `people` that isn't a list refuses (`bad_people`). `when` never does: what can't be read refuses with its own code, as can anything ambiguous (bare `8`, dotted `8.30` under 13:00, a drift word's part against its time such as `tomorrow_morning` at `8pm`, `tomorrow_night` at `1am`, `25:00`, `soon`, `later`, `next_week`, a bare weekday said on that weekday). It computes no dates: the reconciler resolves its relative words in the world's timezone. A clock time with no day word is the next occurrence, as it always was, whatever its format (`8am` and `08:00` at 18:40 both mean tomorrow). A day said out loud that has already gone (`this_morning` at 18:40) fails `out_of_window` and never rolls; "tonight at 12am" is the coming midnight. Each failure is its own counter, shown on Life · Now: `invite_no_when` (nothing stated, which the prompt allows and which stays dropped: filling a time in would change "never invent a time", an owner decision), `invite_bad_time`, `_bad_relative`, `_bad_date`, `_bad_part`, `_bad_when`, `_bad_confidence`, `_bad_type`, `_no_object` and so on. Codes only: the clerk's answer is never logged.
