# Scene play-outs (spec 016)

Detail moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-11-02, the file passed Claude Code's size limit). Keep this file current when the feature changes.

How an outing with someone went. Everything lives in `src/life/story/scenes/`; **only `src/life/story/index.js` imports `scenes/*`** (guard `21-guards` §8). Inert unless `arcs` + life run and `arcs.scenesEnabled` (`ARCS_SCENES_ENABLED`, default true). Design: `specs/016-scene-playouts/{spec,plan,export-contract}.md`.

- **Detect** (`detect.js`, `worker.js scan`) — the story's afterTick (live only, never a sim: scenes are absent from `tomorrow.simulate`, the Day projection and preview tomorrow) scans `activity.ended` past `story_state.scenes_cursor`. The cursor seeds at the log head on the first scan and on every off → on of (arcs ∧ `arcs.scenesEnabled`) (`story_state.scenes_on`, R24): outings from an off period never get a scene. A session = an `activity.started` whose kind is not in `cast.js ROUTINE_ACTIVITY_KINDS`, with an active cast member or a real person (`subjectJid`), ≥ 20 min, not excluded/redacted, no heavy chain → a `pending` row (`UNIQUE (activity, version)`: restart-safe; any row for an outing → never again). Ended > `ARCS_SCENE_MAX_AGE_HOURS` ago → a `skipped:too_old` row and **no event**. The first scenes job after attach resets rows a crash left `running` (v1 → `pending`, or `skipped:interrupted` past the max age; a regenerate → skipped).
- **Play out** (`facts.js`, `charter.js`, `schema.js`, `worker.js runScene`) — before any gate: a **real person** (`mode:'self_only'`) gets a code template about him only (the outing, the place's kind word, the weather, his mood bands at the start; outcome pinned to well/fine, ending nothing, no topics/highlight/awkward/impressions/effects; status `done`, tier `code`, `fallback:false`) and **never a model call** (D10/D17); a **paused Director** or a **heavy stretch / embargo at the session** (raw-log check: `heavy.started` / `embargo.started` at ≤ start with `until` > start, not ended) → the code one-liner (`skip_reason` paused / heavy), no call, no effects. Else a code fact sheet (no chat text, no JIDs, cast as P1…Pn, a venue only by its **kind word** — the model never sees a business's name, D9; his vitals from the checkpoint at the session start; beats whose cause chain reaches a heavy event dropped), stored as `sheet_json` on v1. An owner-directed full scene (not a chat outing, not a real person) also carries `ownerIntent`: the committed option's label, fenced as owner text, with a line that unnamed people in it stay unnamed. A session ≥ 180 min is `long`: the narration cap is min(2 × `arcs.sceneMaxChars`, 2400), up to 9 paragraphs, and the call's `maxTokens` is 1800. Outcome: the owner tone's outcome word (`sceneTones[decision].outcome`, R23) → a 015 owner outcome → one `rngFor(endDate, 'scene:outcome:<activity>')` draw (`badly` ≤ 10 % at drama 1). Tier: `mind` (caller `life.scene.mind`) for first meetings, dates, family occasions, owner-directed outings; `clerk` (`life.scene.clerk`) else and **always for a chat's outing** (`narration_only`, R10). Gates fail closed (strict ledger read over the `life.scene.` prefix — never under `life.arcs.`, so scenes never touch `ARCS_MIND_BUDGET_USD`): `sceneMaxPerDay` both tiers, `sceneBudgetUsd` month-to-date, `sceneChatMaxPerDay` for chat outings (attempts: a row's `caller` is set when its call starts), and for mind `sceneMindMaxPerDay`, < 60 % of the $ cap, `mindBudgetOk('think')`. Degrade: mind → clerk → code one-liner (never blocks the outing). An `invented_name` rejection retries that same tier once with a correction (same caller, same caps); every failed attempt is stored on `skip_reason` (`mind:invented_name,clerk:invented_name`). A minor-age or non-consent failure is not retried. An explicit-register scene is the exception: one `desire` call and one invented-name retry, then the tame one-liner — never the clerk, and Opus is not shown that sheet. Validation: closed keys, caps, labels, outcome consistency, `maxInvented` = 1 per 400 chars, real names / JIDs / URLs refused; every output string has venue names scrubbed to kind words (before the invented-name check: a scrub, not a reject).
- **As of** (plan 020 D1): `stateAtStart` is `projector.asOf`; vitals and weather read the world at the start, "planned next" the world at its end, a resumed outing from before its first part. Per-field audit: `docs/claude/as-of.md`.
- **Before-the-outing facts** (2026-10-31, the first-meeting bug: a daily cast pass during an outing credited its time together + a history line dated at its start, so the 12:00 scene said "not a first meeting") — `worker.stateAtStart(session)` = `projector.stateBeforeEvent(activityEventId)` (a read-only fold of every event with a smaller id from the newest clean snapshot, today's exclusions applied; shares `readOnlyBase` with `vitalsSeries`). firstMeet, the sheet's people (metrics bands, romance, history, names), the code outcome's weights, the previous scene, the job title and `isDate` (`tierOf(…, {before})`) read it; owner intent (tone, outcome, the decision, `ownerDirected`), the place words and the effects read the state now; beats also need an id below the start. A REGENERATE recomputes firstMeet and rebuilds the sheet from that world (v1's frozen list / sheet only when it can't be read), keeps v1's outcome (R16), and its tier moves up to mind when the facts now say so (never down). Test: `arcs-tests/76-scenes-before-facts`.
- **Commit** — `pulse.exclusive → withDecisionLock` and one transaction: `scene.played` (engine, caused by the `activity.started`, codes + `textSha` + `heavyWindow` only) + effects, the row → `done`/`fallback` with the hint it was written with (`tone`, `tone_outcome`) and `heavy_window`. **heavy_window** = a session within a `heavy.started`'s onset − 2 d … onset + 31 d: such a scene reaches only a DM or a call, forever — never a group, the journal or the site. Effects (`effects.js`, full mode = self/owner origin + fictional cast only, controls allowing): `relationship.changed {reason:'scene'}` **caused by the scene** (small/medium steps, ≤ 0.05 per metric per scene, closeness/trust ≤ 0.06 per member per day, none when 015's `outcome.happened` moved them), and two **uncaused companions** naming it in `payload.sceneEventId` (D15): a romance onset (`arc.started`, onsetRoll's gates minus the roll, ≤ 1 per 30 days by the raw log) and `scene.followup` (no cause allowed; `eventLog` checks the named scene is a self/owner full one) — pulse items fit it with `goals.fitSlot` into an ordinary, NOT pre-committed `decision.opened {followupId}`; it lapses at the window end / a departure / social frozen. Chat-origin outings: narration only.
- **Companions** (`effects.companionsToUndo`, `worker.settleCompanions`) — after any owner revert (`story.onOwnerReverted`), inside a regenerate's commit and in every scenes job (rewinds, automatic reverts): companions of a scene that left the log are reverted (`scene_gone`) **only when not lived** — a follow-up whose decision passed its point of no return or whose outing started, or an onset arc that moved past `interested`, is kept. So reverting a scene, its activity, its decision or the re-plan is never refused (`life.sceneRevertProblem` → null) and never takes an outing he went on out of his day; only a direct revert of a lived `scene.followup` is refused. A follow-up whose decision the owner reverted gets `scene.followup_lapsed {reverted}` (never refitted, R26).
- **Owner** — Director `scene.tone {decisionId, tone, outcome}` (outcome well/fine/flat/badly **required**; refused `outcome_conflict` against a 015 outcome, `real_person` for a decision with a real person, `chat_plan` for a chat's) and `outcome {tone}` (the hint takes that outcome) → owner `scene.tone_set {tone, outcome}`. `story.sceneRegenerate(id, {tone, outcome})`: v+1 reusing v1's roll (outcome, firstMeet, the stored sheet) unless a tone with its outcome word is given (then `outcomeBy:'owner'`); also on a **reverted** scene whose outing is still live (no revert step; `scene.regenerated` names it, R32); on success owner `scene.regenerated` + the old one reverted (its companions settled) + the new one caused by the same activity; failure keeps the old one. `story.sceneEditText(id, text)` (no event). Regenerate / edit / tone of a real person's scene → `real_person`.
- **Reads** — prompts: `life.sceneLines(audience, {recentText, imitating})` → ≤ 1 fenced block ≤ 480 chars in the life tail, gated by `eventAllowed` + the subject rule + the heavy_window DM/call rule; none while imitating, under an embargo / over the self-mention budget, in a heavy stretch. The frame names a venue only for a good moment (never with outcome flat/badly, an awkward moment, a bad ending). Dashboard: `story.sceneDetail(id)` (a reverted / superseded version reads its own `scene.played` payload, so the panel keeps when / where / who; `regenerable`, `toneOutcome`, effects with `kept`), `story.scenesBetween`, `castDetail().scenes` (reverted ones listed, `excluded`), `status().scenes` (incl. `chatToday`/`chatCap`), Day spans' `span.scene` and a planned outing span's `decisionId` (its ❖ TONE link). Journal bullets add "how it went" for full, non-heavy_window scenes. Site: `life.publicScenes(eventIds)` (full, live, not heavy_window).
- **Sweep** (`store.sweep` + `store.sweepActivities`, every scenes job when exclusions change and hourly) — a scene out of the log → its row `reverted`, text/detail NULLed (the code sheet stays for a rewrite); an **outing** out of the log (revert, cascade, rewind, redaction) → **every** version `reverted` with text, edit, detail, tone and sheet NULLed, superseded rows included (R9).
- **Dashboard** (`routes/arcs.js`, `components/scene-panel.js`) — `GET /api/arcs/scenes/:id` (row id or `scene.played` id; `sceneOut` closes the facade's shape; a real person reads as `friend` + `realPerson`, never a JID), `POST …/regenerate {tone?, outcome?}` (2/min; a tone without its outcome → 400 `outcome_required`; a refused/failed result → 409 with its code, not audited; the panel words the code and reloads) and `PUT …/text` (10/min, ≤ 1200, or 2400 when the outing ran at least 3 hours), readonly + CSRF, audited `arcs.scene.*` with ids/lengths/the outcome word only. The HOW IT WENT panel edits / reverts only the live version, rewrites a live or a `regenerable` reverted one, and offers a real person's scene REVERT only; Director OUTCOMES takes a tone hint + its outcome where `godView().decisions[].toneable` (chips pick both).
- **Export** (`export-contract.md`) — `publicView` adds `raw.scenes` (live scenes ended in the window: origin, subject and heavy chain from their `scene.played` / `activity.started` rows, fail closed), places' `venue`/`area`, and spans keep their `eventId` (internal) so a day row gets its `sceneId`; `src/public` joins the text from `life.publicScenes` and applies the contract (fictional cast only, never home / heavy / real person; `v: 1` + `scenesSchema: 1`; narration ≤ 9 paragraphs and 2400 characters).
- **Tests** — `arcs-tests/70-scenes-ui` (routes, panel, Director tone, a pin of the real facade names), `71-scenes-live`, `72-scenes-units`, `73-scenes-owner` (D15 companions: a lived follow-up kept through regenerate and an ancestor revert; R26), `74-scenes-guards` (pause, heavy + heavy_window, the v1 roll, callers + chat cap, a restart mid-call, the activity sweep, a reverted scene's panel + rewrite, venue names), `76-scenes-before-facts` (mid-session pass / history / beat / drift leave the before-facts equal; today's live shape → firstMeet + mind; a regenerate recomputes a wrong v1 firstMeet), `77-scenes-owner-intent` (owner intent + long sheet, invented-name retry on the same tier, explicit scenes stay on desire, long edit cap, public 2400), `75-scenes-wake` (an owner outing at 01:30 reached via a wake at 01:15 still gets its scene; the wake's own blocks none), `21-guards` §8; `test:public` §12; `test:migrate` v13 → v14.
- **The moment it ends (plan 020 C4)** — the pulse only folds an end at its next tick, so `afterTick` arms a timer, on the real clock, for an eligible outing ending inside the coming tick (`armSceneKick`; an end already past since the grid point goes at once). It settles the pulse and queues the scenes job. Everything it does is what the next tick would do, so a race with the tick lets one through (the scan's cursor, the row's pending → running). A timer is lost on restart; the next tick picks the outing up. An owner's settle queues the job too. Live only: `afterTick` never runs in a sim.
- **The session's record (plan 020 C4)** — `runScene` reads `liveSessionTrace` (through `deps.liveTrace`), a pure read of the log, so it's the same after a restart. Its outcome is passed as `fixed`, so the scene never re-rolls what the live line showed; an owner's tone outcome still wins. One outing, one scene, at the true end: an `activity.ended` whose block a later start resumes (a re-plan mid-outing, a rewind) is skipped at the scan, and a queued row is dropped if the resume came after (`liveResumedLater`). It's told from where it began, and a tail under 20 min gets none of its own: it's that one scene (`resumedLong`). When the resume comes after an earlier start's scene was written (a cancel undone), the new scene is version + 1 on its own start: `priorLiveScene` finds the old one through `liveEarlierStarts`, the commit reverts it (`scene_resumed`, its effects and unlived companions with it), names it in `payload.regenOf` so stance evidence moves over, and marks its row superseded. Before-facts read from before the anchor (`session.beforeEventId`), so a sheet doesn't remember its own earlier scene. Such a row isn't on the owner's regenerate cap. `recordFor` puts it on the sheet as `sheet.trace` (`traceSheet`: the phases that say something, topics unless the owner has said what this outing is, interruptions, the mood's path; a mood under 10 min is dropped, and his half of it where it disagrees with the outcome; over 24 rows the middle goes first and the ending stays), rendered as "how it went". Precedence in the charter: owner intent, then that as the shape, then don't invent beyond it. While he's still on the way, "afterwards" takes who he's with from the plan in force: the first block at the destination with one of them, within 30 min of the first block there (a wait at the place is its own block); the log's arrival reads the same way, so the stored line stays true. In "afterwards", home and his place are only `self.homePlaceId`; any other home is their place. Never for the explicit register (its own path, sheet unchanged), for minimal mode, or once a regenerate's outcome no longer agrees with it. Real chats are "his phone buzzed", with no count or word. Past the max age it isn't read. Still one call per scene.
- **How things stand + his commitments (plan 020 D2, D3)** — the sheet carries the note that held when the outing began, in place of the history and beats it covers, and any promise with them that came to nothing (a scene written within 6 h); never on an explicit sheet or a chat's outing: `docs/claude/relationship-memory.md`.
- **Plans made face to face (owner 2026-11-05)** — what a scene says they decided really happens. The writer's JSON has an optional closed `plans` (≤ 3; `schema.plansOf`: `kind` outing | visit_his | visit_theirs | errand | chore, `who` labels, `also` first names from "others he knows", `placeHint`, `spot`, `when` 'now' | HH:MM, `day`, `minutes`, `doing`); a bad entry is dropped, never the scene; none on a real person's scene, on a chat's outing (`effectsBlocked`) or a code tier, none with them when it went badly. The sheet's `planning` (only within `PLANS_MAX_AGE_HOURS` (6) of the end) gives the time, his week, his plans a week ahead, other cast names, and `decide`: a code draw on the outing (`DECIDE_NEXT_P` well 0.5, fine 0.2, flat and badly 0) that they do decide something, with a drawn sort of thing (`DECIDE_KINDS`), because left alone the writers either write none or all settle on a meal on Saturday. The two go-on sorts (`GO_ON`) are drawn only within 30 min of the end and with no work or meeting in the next 2 h; they are told plainly ("they do not part here… use when: now") and the sheet then leaves out the just-begun walk home (the writers keep to an `afterwards` line over any hint: 0 of 9 went on until it was gone). The commit wrapper runs `deps.planFromScene` after the scene's own transaction, so a plan that fails never undoes the story. `phone/worker.planFromScene` resolves each through `resolveTextPlan` + `appendPlan` with `by:'scene'` (`COMMIT_BY`, promise weight, locked, later texts move or cancel it like a text plan; "agreed in person" on Life · Day). A refusal about his day leaves the phone's plan note, so he texts them that it won't work. A plan is an uncaused companion naming `sceneEventId` (D15): `companionsToUndo` reverts it when the scene goes, unless he has set off. **Now**: only within 30 min of the end, ≤ 3 a day. Heading home already and they're going to his place: they go with him, starting when he arrives. Anywhere else: `travel.arrived {to: the outing's place}` cuts the trip he just began, and `decision.continuing` makes `pulse.fitLockedOuting` leave from where he stands, no prep time. **New spot**: `spot` makes a generic `place.created` (`place:spot-…`, 0.3–3 km off, ≤ 2 a week, a name it already has reuses that place), and a home for someone who has none as in the phone. **Asked along**: `also` joins the plan's `castIds` and queues one `plan_invite` text from him; they are assumed to be coming, and a no from them doesn't take them off it. Their cancel doesn't call it off for the rest (`not_theirs`). Test `arcs-tests/99-scene-plans`.
