# Wake him ("can't sleep") and the night past midnight

Detail moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-11-02, the file passed Claude Code's size limit). Keep this file current when the feature changes.

The owner can wake him in the night from the Director (SCHEDULE panel → WAKE HIM, or TELL THE STORY: "he can't sleep and goes out…" → a `wake` draft + the outing scene). Tests: `arcs-tests/63-wake-him` (the live night: asleep 00:30 on yesterday's plan → wake 01:15 → outing 01:30 with a new cast member → home → asleep → a later morning wake with the broken night's debt; wake now + REVERT THIS CHANGE; the Tell-the-story drafts; a past-midnight owner outing while still up), `62-revert-rearm` (Bug A).

- **Action** `{kind:'wake', reason:'cant_sleep'|'woken_up'|'early_start', at?:'HH:MM'|unix (default now), until?:'HH:MM'|unix (not early_start)}` (`director.planWake`, route shape check in `routes/director.js`): refused `not_asleep` unless he is asleep at `at` (asleep now and before his own wake, or inside the sleep block of the plan in force), `after_wake` within 10 min of his wake, `wake_open` while another request is unanswered, `bad_until`. One owner `wake.set {at, reason, until?}` (owner-only, chat-forbidden as an exact entry — never a `sleep.` prefix, which would forbid the `sleep.started` a chat-adopted outing reaches; `REVERTIBLE_TYPES`), folded into the lazy `s.wakeSets` (≤ 8, two days). The facade settles the pulse after apply (like a schedule edit), so "now" wakes him at once. Not a god kind (no `GOD_EVENT_TYPES` change); in `PROMPT_ACTION_KINDS`, never in APPLY ALL (`target_resolved`); `projection.PROJECT_KINDS` has it; CHANGES reads "Wake him · can't sleep · up at …".
- **Pulse** (the only place it acts: `items()` returns early while he sleeps): `ownerWakeDue` (asked after he fell asleep, before his own `wakeFor`, unanswered; the earliest) → for `early_start` the normal `wakeUp` at that time (its `woke` caused by the request); else `nightWake`: an engine `woke {interrupted:true, wakeSetId}` caused by the request + an engine `plan.made` (`nightPlan`: same date / wake as the plan in force, the blocks before his sleep, the broken sleep, free at home until `until` or `NIGHT_AWAKE_MIN` (45) — extended to any locked outing tonight — then sleep until his wake, ≥ 60 min; no `basePlanEventId`, so every night block is caused by this plan → the woke → the request). An interrupted `woke` leaves `lastWokeAt` / `lateBy` alone (sets `sleep.interruptedAt`) and settles no debt (vitals): the morning `woke` settles the whole broken night, so the debt follows the vitals model. Revert: before it fires, or a night wake until his morning wake (the cascade takes the woke, the night plan and what he did up); an early start only before it fires (`already_happened`).
- **Bug B — locked outings past midnight**: `pulse` fits a locked outing into the plan IN FORCE at the outing (`plan.wakeAt ≤ eventAt < plan.nextWakeAt`, not the plan's date: the morning plan of the outing's date starts after a 01:30 outing and must not expire it `could_not_go`), and `fitLockedOuting` treats a sleep block he hasn't started (he is awake) before the outing as a stay at home he leaves from (the kept part becomes `free`, never a partial sleep block). Asleep at its point of no return (not in the plan in force) → `decision.expired could_not_go` at the ponr — unless a wake request for that sleep is pending (even one after the ponr: once he is up the fit decides, he goes late or could_not_go). Already asleep at preview → continuity C03 `sleeping` (warn) carries the code suggestion `{kind:'wake', reason:'cant_sleep', at:'HH:MM'}` ("he can't sleep: wake him at HH:MM", 15 min before he must leave, never after the ponr, 5-min grid) when the outing is an owner go; a batch with a wake request for that night (`wokenFor`) no longer warns, and the projection runs the wake, so no `projected_missed`.
- **Bug C — "stays up late" on the next date**: `planEdits.overridesForPlan` also takes a next-date override with a stretch (not sleep) starting before the plan's `nextWakeAt`; `replanToday` fits only its night part (`nightPart`: no background / remove / move); `applyOverride`'s `lateNightInto` moves tonight's sleep (not yet started) to the stretch's end (≥ 60 min of sleep left) before anchoring, so the stretch is no longer clamped away at bedtime.
- **Bug A — revert re-arms a restored outing**: `projector.noteExcluded` → `liftRestored`: an excluded `decision.expired` drops the `plan.made` `amended:<decisionId>` exclusion signatures noted before it, so the pulse fits / amends the restored decision again (in the same process); `revertChangePreview` lets the change's own `event.reverted` part (CANCEL TONIGHT reverting the fitted plan) ride along with the cancel instead of refusing the whole change.
