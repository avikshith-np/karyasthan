# His wallet (spec 018)

Detail moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-11-02, the file passed Claude Code's size limit). Keep this file current when the feature changes.

What he earns and spends, itemized. Design: `specs/018-wallet/plan.md`. Engine + export (the Life · Wallet page is its own note).

- **Cut-over** — before 018 the vitals drained `world.money.monthlyBaseline` smoothly per hour and only trips (and heavy setbacks) were `money.spent`. The pulse now appends one engine `wallet.started` (chat-forbidden, quiet on Life · Day, neutral for 014's A7) at its first step; the projector keeps `s.money.walletSince` and passes `itemized` to `vitals.integrate`, so the hourly drain stops from then on and a pre-018 log integrates byte for byte up to it.
- **Spends** (`src/life/spending.js`, code only, `rngFor(date,'spend:…')` jitter, rounded to 10; shares of the price level = the baseline, else salary × 0.45): `outing` (an activity of a non-routine kind at a place that isn't a home — a committed option's own `spend` first, else `OUTING_SHARE` by kind / `PLACE_KIND_SHARE` by place kind; a walk, the gym, a park, a visit are free) and `food` (lunch at the office = the canteen, a meal / café / restaurant away from home), both caused by their `activity.started` (a chat outing's spend keeps the chat origin; reverting the activity takes it); an outing is paid for once — a resumed block (a rewind or re-anchor mid-outing leaves him idle and the pulse starts the same block again) is not charged while an earlier `activity.started` of it (same kind, place and company, overlapping window, since the day's plan) and its `money.spent` are both still in the projection (`pulse` `chargedBefore`; rewound / reverted away → the resume pays; test `life-tests/61-spend-once`); `groceries` (a groceries trip's top-up, and the weekly basket at wake on Sunday, catch-up after `GROCERIES_CATCHUP_DAYS`); `rent` (the 1st) and `bills` (the 10th) at wake. Payload `{amount, for, kind?, placeId?, castIds?, decisionId?, period?}`; optional `world.money.rent` / `bills` / `groceriesWeekly` give exact amounts. A 31-day dry run with salary 175000 / baseline 78000 spends ~63000 (rent 31200, groceries 11900, food 11200, bills 8100, travel 700).
- **Salary** — paid at the first wake on or after the payday (clamped to the month's length) while that month is unpaid and the world existed on that day (genesis date before it, or the wake is on it): a downtime over the payday no longer loses the month; no back-pay for a month before genesis. `money.salary` carries `month`; rent / bills / baskets carry `period` (`s.money.paid`). A reverted salary or recurring spend stays undone for its period (`exclusionSig` `salary:<month>` / `<for>:<period>`).
- **Seam** — `spending.__setWalletForTest(false)` runs the pre-018 engine (no cut-over, no new spends, the exact-day salary rule): the 012 goldens (`chat-influence-tests/_golden.js`) and `life-tests/27-pulse-story` use it.
- **walletView** (`src/life/wallet.js`, pure; facade `life.walletView({month})`, `WalletError` `bad_month` / `out_of_range`) → `{running, month, months, balance, currencyHint: null, salary {amount, payday, lastAt, nextAt}, baseline, totals {in, out, net, byCategory}, daily [{date, in, out, balance}] (every day from the 1st or genesis to today; balances from one read-only `vitalsSeries`), entries [{id, at, dir, amount > 0, category, label, placeId, placeName, castIds, castNames, origin, fromChat, actor, revertible}] (newest first, ≤ 500)}`. Categories: salary income rent bills groceries food outing travel setback adjustment other; `byCategory.adjustment` is signed (an owner "set" by how far it moved him). Labels are code words (`labelOf`), except an owner money change shows its own `label` (dashboard only).
- **Public** (013 extended, owner request) — `publicView` reads `WALLET_TYPES` under the same filter: listed only when self / owner origin, no subject, not redacted / excluded / heavy, no heavy cause; a chat / subject spend → that day's `outWithFriends` (no time, place, kind or label); setbacks and owner "set"s never; code-word labels only (never the owner's label); a home-like place never an item's place. `public.json` gains the optional `wallet` + `walletSchema: 1` beside `"v": 1` (the live site refuses any other v): month, balance, salary (dates), monthIn / monthOut, closed `byCategory` (+ `out`), every window day newest first with ≤ 400 `w_…` items. Contract: `specs/013-public-site/contracts/public-json.md`. Tests: `life-tests/60-wallet`, `test:public` §13.
- **Life · Wallet** (`#life/wallet[/<YYYY-MM>]`, `pages/life-wallet.js`, guide `guides/life-wallet.js`) — `GET /api/life/wallet?month=YYYY-MM` (`routes/life.js`; 400 bad month / `bad_month`, 404 `out_of_range`, 200 `{running:false}` while life is off like every life read, 409 `not_available` only without the facade): balance, salary + next payday, month in / out / net, by-category bars, the month's balance line (inline SVG), the log (◇ CHAT / OWNER marks, names from `castNames`, REVERT through the life revert route when `revertible`), month picker; grouped numbers only, no currency symbol. Test: `test:dashboard` `wallet:` block (stub seam `__setWalletForTest` in `routes/life.js`).
