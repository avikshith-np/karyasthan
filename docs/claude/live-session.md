# The live outing (plan 020 C1–C2)

While he is out with someone: what is going on right now, as a state and a line. Keep this file current when the feature changes.

- **A read of the log.** `src/life/liveSession.js` (pure), `src/life/livePhrases.js` (the words), imported by the life facade alone. No model call, no storage, no world event: a revert or a rewind is exact at once, and a restart reads the same line. Never called from a simulation.
- **What is a session.** The scene's own rule, through the story seam `story.liveContext` → `scenes/worker.liveContext`: a non-routine kind, with someone, ≥ 20 minutes by its plan, no heavy chain. Only at the venue (a trip before or after is no session). A resumed block (same kind, place, company, overlapping window, after a rewind) is one session from its first live start, so it does not start arriving again.
- **`life.liveSession({at})`** → `{running, active}` and, on an outing, `{mode}`. Full: `phase` (`arriving settling main lull winding leaving`), `phaseWords`, `minutesIn`, `minutesLeft`, `his {mood, energy, sick}` (bands), `them [{castId, name, mood}]`, `attention` and `closeness` `{band, value}`, `topic {code, words, since}`, `interruptions`, `line {at, parts}`, `text`, `private?`. Route `GET /api/life/live`; the line on Life · Now uses `components/views.js liveLine`.
- **Minimal mode** — a real person (`real_person`), a chat's outing (`chat`), a heavy stretch or embargo (`heavy`), or the heavy window (`heavy_window`): the line "out with a friend" and nothing else. No JID, place, mood or topic exists in the output.
- **Private form** — an explicit-register session (`sceneRegister`, read only): the place and a few neutral words, no topic, no phone line. The phrase table holds nothing sexual, at any heat, for anyone (`life-tests/74` scans it); the explicit scene at the end is the desire feature's, untouched.
- **How it goes.** `outcome` is the scene's own: `contextFor` gives the owner's tone outcome word, else a 015 owner outcome, else the same seeded draw, so the line and the end scene agree. **An owner tone shapes the mood through its outcome word only; its words never appear.** Her mood runs from a start (closeness, tension) toward the outcome's level, with a small seeded wobble. Going flat or badly means more lulls and an earlier wind-down; tired too.
- **Interruptions.** `phone.message` from anyone not there, and `chat.load` ending well into the session, become moodlets: attention and closeness dip and fade in 10–20 minutes (seeded per event), capped. Real chats are counts only. Whether he answered is read from the log (`phone` decides); nothing is decided here. Display only: no relationship or vital moves.
- **Topics.** Codes seeded per session from what is really in their lives (the member's beats, job, home town, shared history), then ordinary talk; `TOPIC_WORDS` gives the words. There are no per-cast interests in the state yet: plan 020 B would add them here.
- **Words.** The walk is one step per 5 minutes from the start; every pick is `rngFor(<start's local date>, 'live:<activity>:…')`, and replaying the walk is the repeat memory (a detail doesn't come back for about 3 hours). A detail holds 10–15 minutes, then gives way to another or to nothing. What it may say depends on the time of day, the kind of place, rain, first meeting, whether he has a job, sick, tired, two or more people.
- **`life.liveSessionTrace(activityEventId)`** — for the end scene's sheet: any start of the outing → `null` (no session, gone from the log, over in under 20 minutes) | `{mode:'minimal', reason, activityEventId}` | `{mode:'full', private?, activityEventId, startAt, endAt, minutes, outcome, outcomeBy, late, phases [{phase, from, to}], topics [{code, words, from, to, back?}] (≤ 12), mood [{at, them, his}] (changes, ≤ 16), attention {mean, low}, interruptions [{at, kind text|answered|chats|chats_sent, who?, sent?, received?, answered?}] (≤ 20)}`. Fictional names and codes only.
- **Tests** — `life-tests/74-live-lines` (phases, moods, moodlets, repeat memory over 4 hours, every phrase for every pronoun, what the table may never say, what fits the moment), `arcs-tests/99-live-session` (the trip, revert, rewind resume, midnight, restart, two people, the flatmate at home, short, late, tone, sleep, what may be shown), `test:dashboard` `live:`.
