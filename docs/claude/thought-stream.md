# The thought stream (plan 020 F2)

What passes through his head, in code. Keep this file current when the feature changes.

- **Where.** `src/life/thoughts.js` (the rules, pure), `src/life/thoughtPhrases.js` (the bank), `src/life/thoughtStore.js` (the only SQL on `story_thoughts`, migration v16). The life facade alone imports them. From the facade's live `afterTick` only, so never in a simulation.
- **Changes nothing.** No model call, no world event, no state. No decision and no prompt builder reads a thought. `arcs-tests/99-thought-stream` runs the same 3 days with and without it (`__setThoughtsForTest`) and compares the log byte for byte.
- **The competition.** Each tick: candidates from what happened in the last 45 minutes and from his state, scored salience × decay × novelty × a family cooldown, plus a little chance. How many surface: 0–2, drawn from a rate per activity (`RATE`, home ≈ 5 an hour, a meeting ≈ 1.6, a heavy stretch 1), at most 6 an hour, at least 4 minutes apart. A thought with salience ≥ 0.72 surfaces even in a tick that drew silence. Two in a tick come from two families.
- **Families.** `reaction` (rain, a cancel, a scene, money, waking, and a little of whatever he's doing), `want_should` (a lean against a commitment, tired before a plan, the mind's intentions via `mind.openIntentions`, codes only), `putting_off` (an open decision, an unanswered text, bedtime, chores), `body` (vitals), `people` (a text, a bond that moved, someone close, someone in the room, an outing's phase and mood), `wordless` (a feeling, an image, nothing; a chat's mark), `excuse` (right after a choice against his lean, from the thought-vs-deed reader: what he tells himself in `text`, the true cause in `cause_code`).
- **Memory.** Read from the table: a phrase is out for 3 days, the least used of a pool goes first, a family just heard from counts less. A candidate from evidence has a `dedupe_key` on that evidence; an ambient one on its tick's slot; `codes.tick` makes a tick run again (a restart) a no-op.
- **Sweeps.** A thought whose evidence is reverted, rewound or redacted is hidden from the read at once and deleted at the next tick (whenever the exclusions change). `as_of_event_id` (the log's head) takes a thought with no evidence when a rewind covers it. 006's hooks: a forget, revoke or edit deletes that chat's thoughts from the message on (`deleteFromChat`, 5 minutes of skew); a purge deletes all of that person's and errors roll it back. A real forget or revoke mostly arrives as a reverted pointer (012's hooks, then the sweep); the hook is for chat-origin thoughts with no event of their own (intentions).
- **Downtime.** A catch-up tick gives at most one, from the last 45 minutes only.
- **Privacy.** From a chat: a code-built feeling, `origin_kind` + `origin_jid` on their columns (the read says only `fromChat`). A plan with a real person or made in a chat reads "plans with a friend". An outing of a real person or a chat: plain words, no names. A private (explicit-register) session: a few neutral words, nothing else. Nothing sexual in the bank, at any heat (`life-tests/75` scans it). A heavy stretch: his body and a few plain words, a low rate, nothing dark invented (the same test scans for it), and evidence with a heavy chain never counts.
- **On an outing.** The live session's phase, mood and who's there choose the words; chores, work and bedtime are silent while he's out.
- **Known limits.** The sweep reads every row and its evidence whenever the exclusions change: fine at this size; cap it or restrict by `as_of_event_id` if it ever shows in a tick. Wordless warm / flat read the view's vitals, which hold 012's chat-mood overlay, so a chat can colour a feeling that isn't marked `fromChat`: the overlay is felt everywhere by design, and no word or name can come through it. A crash between two inserts of one tick loses the second, never doubles it.
- **Retention.** 180 days, like `llm_usage`, pruned on the first tick of each local day. About 50 short rows a day (at most ~96), so about 9–17k rows at most.
- **Dashboard only.** `GET /api/life/thoughts?date=&limit=` → `{running, date, today, count, thoughts [{id, at, family, kind thought|body|feeling, text, cause, fromChat}]}`. ON HIS MIND on Life · Now (newest 8) and Life · Day (the day), through the kit's `stream`. Not exported to the site.
- **Not yet.** The mixed-language voice comes later through phrase banks (H2).
- **Real thoughts (F3)** share the table but not the stream: `docs/claude/real-thoughts.md`. The stream's memory, rate and the site leave them out (`isRealThought`).
- **Tests.** `life-tests/75-thought-rules`, `arcs-tests/99-thought-stream`, `test:migrate` (v16), `test:dashboard` `thoughts:`.
