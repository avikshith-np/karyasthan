# Director as god (spec 015)

The owner may rewrite his future from Life · Director → PLAY GOD (`pages/life-director-god.js`, read `GET /api/director/god` → story facade `godView()`), and every Director preview is checked for continuity (a break is a WARNING + a suggestion, never a refusal). Design: `specs/015-god-director/plan.md` (owner decisions at its end override the body). Built on 014: `places.worldWithPlaces` (inside `worldOverlay`), `places.placeLabel` / `isRealVenue` / `isPrivatePlace` (+ every former home in `self.homeHistory`) / `NEGATIVE_TYPES` (+ `outcome.happened {badly}`, `decision.expired {fell_through}`, `career.set {fire|quit}`, `money.adjusted {expense}`), `planEdits.replanToday(…, {dropDecisionIds})` (an outing called off: its blocks refilled from the routine) / `{rebase:true}` (career, home or sick change: the rest of today from a fresh `planDay` under the new overlay, kept outings anchored), `tomorrow.simulate(…, {descriptors, anchorFirst, closeChatMood, appendDescriptors, deadlineAt})` for the projection, and 014's `'off'` day type (unemployed weekdays; a work background in a schedule edit has no job to apply to).

- **Actions** (`director.js`, all preview → apply, owner events caused by the `director.action`, engine consequences caused by their owner event): `decision.pick` (an owner commit / reversal; the projector keeps `by:'owner'` and only an owner event may reverse it, no tension), `outcome` (well / badly → engine `outcome.happened` + `relationship.changed {reason:'outcome'}` once the outing is over; cancelled → engine `decision.expired {fell_through}` + an engine re-plan without it), `career` (hire / fire / quit / promote / job / salary / pattern, now or `when` later; out of work: no office, weekday `dayType 'off'`, weekend wake, no salary, appraisal skips, a hunt starts `unemployed`; never a real business as employer or office — a venue-like place (real or owner-renamed) as `officePlaceId` or a new office / title / employer named like any venue ever recorded → `real_business`, and the prompt validator leaves a catalogue venue id out of a career draft (`check.venuePlaceIds`); never model-drafted employer names), `money` (set / income / expense, exact at fold), `home` (near a known place or into an `area` from the HOME tab's AREA search; a `place:home-<n>` at 0.01°, `self.moved`, flatmate stays / leaves / comes; the overlay moves routine leisure at any former home — TV — to the new one), `mood` (preset or capped delta at a later time or a wake day — `deps.atWake` pulse seam appends it before that morning's plan), `health` (sick now / later 1–14 days, well), `arc.end`, `cast.depart` / `cast.return` (departed members move to `story.departed`: off cards, meetings, block company and together credit; names still resolve; ids never reused; a depart expires only their plans still to come — one already over keeps its outcome — and re-plans today without one that was in today's plan; a chat-made plan is kept with `chat_plan_kept` marked `fromChat`, never shown to the story read), `cast.metrics` with intimacy / passion / commitment.
- **Events** (`eventLog.GOD_EVENT_TYPES`, all chat-forbidden, none `director.*` so a rewind removes them): owner-only `outcome.set`, `career.set`, `self.moved`, `money.adjusted`, `vitals.planned`, `sick.planned`, `cast.departed`, `cast.returned`; engine `outcome.happened` (cause `outcome.set`), `change.applied` (cause `career.set`/`self.moved`). `decision.expired {fell_through|career_changed|cast_departed}` only from the engine with the matching cause type; payload `by:'owner'` only from the owner. Scheduled changes fold into `story.pending` and fire from `story/pending.js` (story items; never an owner event); `story.outcomes` holds outcomes. New slices are lazy (never in `initialStory()`), so pre-015 logs fold byte-identically. `reducer.overlayKey(s)` (career / home / departure) gates every overlay site; `driftKey` re-plans today once after a plan-changing god change (`overlay:<key>` exclusion signature).

A scene whose `scene.played` came back this way (its words already swept) can be rewritten like a live one. Test: `arcs-tests/98-revert-plan-barrier`.
