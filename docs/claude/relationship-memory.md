# How things stand + his commitments (plan 020 D2, D3)

Structure before similarity: what still holds, per person, read as of a moment, instead of dumping history at the writers. Design: `specs/020-living-being/plan.md` §D. Keep this file current when the feature changes.

## How things stand (D2)

A note of at most 600 characters per **fictional** cast member. Real people are not in this slice.

- **Where** — `src/life/story/standing/`, imported only by `story/index.js` (`21-guards` §10): `store.js` (the only door to `story_standing` and `story_standing_evidence`, migration v19, adds only), `facts.js` (pure: events → dated lines), `charter.js` (the clerk's charter, its input, the output check), `worker.js` (pass, sweep, reads). Appends no world event, so the 012 goldens are untouched.
- **A version** — text, its own evidence event ids, `as_of_event_id` (everything up to here was considered), `valid_from` / `valid_to`, `supersedes` (the version it was built on), status `live` | `superseded` | `invalidated`. A new one closes the old one; an old one is invalidated, never edited. At most one live per member (partial unique index).
- **When** — the mind's sleep step 2c: `life.storyStandingPass` → `story.standingPass` → `worker.runPass`, feature-detected, in a try, never changes the night's status. It runs before reflection, so a failed reflection can't stop it, and a retried night calls nothing for what's already done. With the mind skill off or paused no sleep runs, so nothing is rebuilt; the note stays and the sheets keep reading it, dated.
- **Who** — active members with something new since their version's as-of. `changedEnough`: a scene, a plan, a beat, a romance step, a move of ≥ 0.03 for a reason that's news (`owner`, `outcome`, `scene`, `decision`, `arc`), or two or more texts. Daily drift, however big, and one passing text wait. A plain "no" to a passing idea isn't a line. Order: romance partner, what happened (scenes and romance weigh most), closeness, id.
- **What it reads** — only self / owner-origin events with no subject, not excluded or redacted, not heavy, no heavy in the chain, outside every heavy window (onset − 2 d … onset + 31 d, or the stretch's end if later): plans (`decision.committed/reversed/expired`, `outcome.happened`, never a chat-made one), scenes in their words (full mode, not heavy_window; highlight + the first 300 characters), texts in their words (live, ≤ 110 each), beats, relationship moves, romance steps. Newest kept per source (`CAPS`: 6 scenes, 12 texts, 10 plans, 6 beats, 6 moves, 6 romance), 60 days back at most, so the call is the same size after six months of romance. Places are kind words; venue names scrubbed. A beat from before they last met in person (any activity with them) is marked "(before they last met)", and the charter says such news is over unless it's a lasting change: "she's planning that evening" must not outlive the evening. **Explicit words never reach the clerk**: a text on the explicit register (`phone.message.register`, a code word beside `heat`), with explicit heat, a naughty tone, a word-check hit, or an old row (no register) with heat `suggestive`, is "(private)" (`isPrivateTurn`: the word list is English only, so it can't be the rule; a flirty text on the tame register stays readable); a scene with `heat: 'explicit'`, on the explicit register, or with explicit words is its frame only.
- **The call** — `clerk` role, caller `mind.standing`, one per member, input ≤ 8 KB: who they are (bands, romance stage), the previous note, what's new. From the sleep step it goes through the mind's `clerkCall`, so it counts under `MIND_CLERK_MAX_PER_DAY` and shows on the Mind page as a digest-kind run with codes only.
- **Caps, all fail closed** — `ARCS_STANDING_ENABLED`, `ARCS_STANDING_MAX_PER_NIGHT` (members, default 4; the rest wait their turn, so a first night across 7 people takes two nights), a day cap of twice that from the ledger (a member may take a second try), `ARCS_STANDING_BUDGET_USD` month to date over the `mind.standing` prefix, `mindBudgetOk('sleep')`'s answer, the mind's clerk cap, the role's own budget (`skipped`), a ledger or real-names read that fails. Any stop, a failed call or a bad answer leaves the old version live.
- **Output check** (`validateStanding`) — closed JSON `{standing}`; venue names → kind words; over 600 cut at a sentence; identifiers, URLs, real names, explicit words and three words in a row of a private turn (`echoesPrivate`, `private_words`, the word list can't see his mixed speech) refused and the old version kept; an invented capitalised word (zero allowed, unlike the writers' tolerance; a sentence's first word only if it isn't a common opener, so a false alarm costs a call, never a wrong note) or a day word (today, tomorrow, last night…: it's read days later) gets **one** retry naming the word, else the old version stays. A revert during the call, of the lines read or of the base, drops the result (`stale`).
- **Undo** — `sweep()` runs on the story's afterTick when the exclusions or the newest heavy onset change, and deep at the night's pass. A version goes if its evidence left the log (`evidence_gone`), its as-of fell in a rewound range (`rewound`), its evidence is inside a heavy window that began later (`heavy_window`), it was built on one that went (`built_on_gone`), or, deep, an owner edited the words behind it (`words_changed`: edits append no event, so `words_sig` catches them). At the next pass the newest clean version stands again, word for word, with no call (`restored`), even if a budget stops the rest; then it's rebuilt from there if anything still in his life is new. Between the undo and that pass `noteAt` checks the whole chain against the log on every read and answers none: the sheet falls back to its history.
- **The sheets** — `noteAt(s, castId, at)`: the version that held at `at` (a scene: at its start, the same rule as `stateAtStart`; never a note written after the outing). Absent → the sheet is byte for byte as before. Present: phone (his side) `them.standing` + `latelyAfter`, scene `people[].standing` + `historyAfter` + `beatsAfter`, labelled by `asOfAt` (the time of its as-of event, not the row's `valid_from`: a restored note is old words on a new row): the beats it covers are dropped, newer ones still show. The shared history stays on the scene sheet, whatever its date: the note never reads it (`READ_TYPES` has no `cast.history_added`), so `historyAfter` is the whole list and is only cut at the sheet's own caps. `lastTime` and "his recent days" stay: they're specific, the note isn't. It renders in its own fence (a fact line is cut at 400), dated, and says the fact sheet wins. Caps: phone cuts the thread to 3, then drops the note and commitments, then the thread further; scene drops them with the rest at its second level. **An explicit sheet stays as it was.** **His side of the phone only** (`memoryFor`, and `buildSheet` ignores it for `side:'cast'`): the note is his view and draws on moves like "less trust", which `FACTS_CAST` doesn't let her know, so the cast sheet renders byte for byte as with no note, its `lately` untouched. A scene's narrator does read it (third person, not speaking as anyone, though it writes what she says and `impressions.them`).
- **Dashboard** — People member page, HOW THINGS STAND (the note, as-of, evidence count, earlier versions with why they went): `castDetail().standing` → `GET /api/arcs/cast/:id`. A member who left isn't openable there (the page's rule); their rows stay in the table and return to the sheets when they come back.
- **Departed** — no call, `noteAt` none, not on the site rows; the note stays. On a return the next pass catches up from its as-of.
- **Site** — `life.publicStanding()` (live notes of members he has now, holding now — a revert of any evidence drops it at once, before the sweep — not written in a heavy window) → `src/public/index.js standingRows` → `build.js buildStanding` (one row per member of the document, venue words, home and region check, scrubber, nothing near a heavy onset or in a hard stretch) → the optional `standing` key + `standingSchema: 1` beside `"v": 1`, absent with nothing to say. Contract in `docs/claude/public-site.md`.

### Limits

Versions aren't pruned (≤ 24 rows a night at the cap, a small table; a chain walk per sheet read). A note is about the two of them only; it can't see a chat, so a plan a chat made is simply absent from it. An owner edit of words inside an older version of the chain isn't noticed, only the live version's own.

## His commitments (D3)

`src/life/commitments.js`, pure, no storage, no model, nothing appended. `story.commitmentsFor(s, at, tz)` does the reads (decisions 30 days, moves 14, the phone's strong cues) and works with arcs off; `life.commitmentsView()` → `GET /api/life/commitments` → Life · Now, HIS COMMITMENTS.

- **A row** — `{key, who, castIds, what, when, source decision|invitation|claim|goal|text, by, weight (planWeight: owner 3, promise 2, wish 1), status, fromChat, flag, covered, code, eventIds}`. Status: `overdue`, `no_plan`, `open`, `on`, then `kept`, `broken`, `lapsed` for a week after they closed. A go or an invitation older than a week from its own time is gone, whatever became of it (the moves' read reaches 14 days, the decisions' 30). Most pressing first.
- **What counts** — a yes (go / late) on any decision, an invitation still open, a yes taken back (`broken`), `commitment.recorded`, goals (a fitted one is its decision's row). Only leaning, or a plain no, isn't a commitment.
- **Privacy** — who is a fictional name or "a friend". A plan made in a chat (`chatMade`, or a chat-origin / subject `commitment.recorded`) is "a plan from a chat", with no label, place, chat or JID on the row, even when a cast member is named on it.
- **A text with no plan** — `phone/plans.js promiseCue`: the strong cues plus "see you Friday" and friends, a code word and the day, never the words; `promiseDayEnd` gives the day's end (a weekday said on that weekday means the next one). Newest per person, dropped if a plan with them is near it (the sweep's window, stretched to the named day) or they met since, else flagged `no_plan` with the plan step's code from the `phone_plan_outcomes` ring (`planStepOn`, moved to `decisionLog.js`): its own reason, `never_looked`, a failure, or `unknown` when older than the ring. With no day named, any plan with them up to a week on answers it (the phone's "his plans" horizon); one that fell through doesn't. "Next Friday" is that day in the coming Monday-to-Sunday week. Past its named day it's `overdue` for a week. It can't tell a later text called it off, and only reads the plain-language phrases.
- **On the writers' sheets** — `commitmentLines`, ≤ 4 lines, under "his commitments" (phone) or on the person (scene, only when it's written within 6 h of the outing), only what "his plans" doesn't already say: a promise with this person with nothing behind it, one that never came off, and on his side only what he means to do (a goal) or backed out of. Someone else's never. A chat's plan is at most "maybe busy (other plans)", on the phone, when open and within 36 h; committed ones are "his plans"' "busy (other plans)". Never on an explicit sheet.
- **The sweep** — `open_promise` in `sweep.js`: see `docs/claude/sweep.md`.
